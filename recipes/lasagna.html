Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a comforting and rich Italian classic made by layering wide, flat pasta sheets with a slow-simmered, savory ragù of ground meat and tomatoes, a creamy bechamel sauce, and plenty of melted cheeses like mozzarella, ricotta, and Parmesan. As it bakes in the oven, these distinct layers fuse into a bubbling, golden-brown dish with crisp edges and a warm, velvety center that offers a deeply satisfying blend of hearty, cheesy, and tangy flavors in every bite.

Ingredients

  • 500 grams of mince
  • 1 onion
  • 1 garlic
  • tin tomatoes
  • lasagna sheets
  • shredded cheese

Steps

  1. Chop the onions and dice garlic
  2. Put pan on medium heat and cook garlic until aromatic
  3. Brown mince and add tomatoes
  4. cook on low heat until it thinks
  5. while the sauce is cooking, boil lasagna sheets until just tender
  6. in baking tray add spoonful of mince, lasagna sheet and white sauce. Repeat until all ingredients are used.
  7. bake in 200 degrees oven for 20 minutes.
