Pumpkin Soup

Description

Pumpkin soup is a velvety, comforting dish made by puréeing roasted or simmered pumpkin with aromatic ingredients like onions, garlic, and savory broth. Known for its vibrant golden-orange color and naturally sweet, nutty flavor profile, it is often enriched with heavy cream, coconut milk, or melted butter to create a rich, silky texture. Frequently seasoned with warming spices such as nutmeg, cinnamon, or ginger—or elevated with fresh herbs like sage and thyme—this autumn staple is typically served hot, garnished with toasted pumpkin seeds, a swirl of cream, or crisp croutons.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups chopped yellow onion
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 cups pumpkin puree
  • 4 cups vegetable or chicken stock
  • ¾ teaspoon salt
  • ½ cup heavy whipping cream
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a large pot over medium-high; add onion and cook, stirring occasionally, until brown and soft, about 10 minutes. Add peppercorns, garlic, and thyme. Cook, stirring often, until fragrant and slightly brown, about 2 minutes.
  2. Stir in pumpkin puree; cook, stirring occasionally, until puree turns a few shades darker, about 5 minutes. Stir in stock, maple syrup, salt, nutmeg, and cinnamon. Simmer, uncovered, on medium-low heat, stirring occasionally, until slightly reduced, about 30 minutes.
  3. Transfer soup to a blender in batches; secure lid (hold down with a kitchen towel), and blend until smooth, about 30 seconds per batch.
  4. Return soup to the pot; stir in heavy cream. Cook over medium heat until heated through, about 4 minutes.
  5. Ladle soup into bowls and garnish with fresh parsley.
